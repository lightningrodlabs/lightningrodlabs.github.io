    Catching up...

    Eric Harris-Braun · September 11, 2026

    We haven't posted anything here in 4 years. Yow. That's a bit of dereliction of duty. But it has been a long-time problem of mine that I like building so much more than talking about what I've built. So, this is a bit of a catch-up post, just to summarize what's happened here over these years. Thank goodness it's easy to ask an LLM to find the first commit of every repo!

    As you can see below, our focus has landed on Moss. It started out with We and has just grown from there. But since we're a lab, there's been no shortage of experiments, with many more to come.
